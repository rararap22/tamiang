Tamiang Elevator-Escalator Sends Aceh Tamiang’s Best Sons for Training in China

PT. Tamiang Elevator and Escalator sent its best sons, Muhammad Fitra and Ngalim, to attend training and development programs for product and software application system development in Shanghai and several other cities in the People’s Republic of China. Muhammad Fitra (27) is a graduate of State Vocational High School (SMK) 2 Karang Baru in Aceh Tamiang. With his diligence and perseverance, despite being an SMK graduate, Muhammad Fitra successfully secured a prominent position at PT Tamiang Elevator and Escalator as the Head of Research and Development in the Software Development and Technical Application Field. In contrast, Ngalim’s childhood was unlike most people; he could only pursue education until the first year of junior high school. Becoming a successful person is the dream of every human being. However, the journey to success is not easy. It involves effort, hard work, and overcoming challenges. During their time in China, Muhammad Fitra and Ngalim participated in software development training at one of PT. Tamiang Elevator and Escalator’s partner companies in China. Throughout the training, they were directly guided by the company’s leader, Mr. Leo. Meanwhile, CEO of Tamiang Elevator and Escalator, Sugianto, SE, MM, stated that sending Muhammad Fitra and Ngalim for training in China is a manifestation of the company’s commitment to enhancing its employees’ capacities for software development and innovation at PT. Tamiang Elevator and Escalator. Sugianto explained that in his company, education is not the primary measure for placing someone in a position. The first indicators are perseverance, hard work, honesty, a sense of responsibility, and dedication to the company.

Tamiang Elevator Present at JITEX 2024!

Jakarta, August 10, 2024 – Tamiang Elevator proudly participated in the Jakarta International Investment, Trade, Tourism and SME Expo (JITEX) 2024, held at the Jakarta Convention Center (JCC) on August 8, 9, and 10, 2024. This prestigious event was officially opened by Sri Haryati, Secretariat of the Jakarta Provincial Government, along with the Minister of Environment and Forestry of the Republic of Indonesia, Siti Nurbaya, and the Acting Governor of Jakarta, Heru Budi Hartono. JITEX 2024 serves as a significant platform that brings together various industry players, including investors, entrepreneurs, and stakeholders from diverse sectors. At this event, Tamiang Elevator showcased its latest innovations and premium solutions in the field of elevators and escalators, designed to support the development of modern infrastructure in Indonesia. Tamiang Elevator’s presence at JITEX 2024 not only as a participant but also as a company ready to support the growth of the creative economy and tourism in Jakarta and throughout Indonesia. The Tamiang Elevator booth attracted considerable attention from visitors, including investors and entrepreneurs interested in innovative and sustainable vertical transportation solutions. During the three-day event, Tamiang Elevator received enthusiastic responses from both domestic and international visitors. Tamiang Elevator’s participation in JITEX 2024 underscores the company’s commitment to continue innovating and contributing to the development of a more advanced Indonesia.

Meeting of the Member Forum of the National Association of Indonesian Consultants – Aceh Province in 2024

The Executive Committee of the Aceh Branch of the National Association of Indonesian Consultants (Inkindo) held a member forum (Fora) at the Hermes Palace Hotel, Banda Aceh, on Thursday, July 18, 2024. In his speech, the Chairman of the Aceh Provincial Board of Inkindo, Safri Ismi, expressed his gratitude to the Governor of Aceh, who was represented by the Head of the Development Administration Bureau of the Aceh Regional Secretariat, T. Robby Irza. Safri further explained that the forum aimed to discuss various programs and identify issues frequently faced by consultants in the field. Tamiang Elevator participated in the event as part of its commitment to supporting infrastructure development in Aceh. Tamiang Elevator’s participation in this forum not only demonstrated the company’s support for consultants but also provided an opportunity to share experiences and solutions related to elevator and escalator technology that can support more efficient and safer construction projects. Tamiang Elevator’s involvement is expected to make a positive contribution to improving the quality and safety of development projects in Aceh.

Tamiang Elevator Participates in INKINDO’s 45th Anniversary Event

Jakarta, June 20, 2024 – The National Association of Indonesian Consultants (INKINDO) is organizing a series of activities to celebrate its 45th Anniversary. These activities will focus on major themes aligned with the upcoming Government Cabinet’s Vision of Golden Indonesia 2045. This vision includes several important sub-themes, such as downstream industry development, energy conservation, human resource development, food security, and infrastructure development. As part of these activities, Tamiang Elevator is actively participating. As a company committed to innovation and sustainability, Tamiang Elevator will showcase their latest solutions in energy-efficient vertical transportation. This participation is not only to promote their latest technology but also to support the government’s initiative in enhancing national infrastructure quality. In addition to showcasing their flagship products and technologies, Tamiang Elevator will also deliver a presentation on the importance of energy conservation in the use of elevators and escalators. By offering energy-efficient solutions, Tamiang Elevator hopes to contribute to national energy security efforts and support sustainable infrastructure development. This event is expected to strengthen the relationship between industry players and the government, fostering closer collaboration to achieve the Vision of Golden Indonesia 2045. Through their participation in this event, Tamiang Elevator demonstrates its commitment to supporting human resource development, food security, and high-quality infrastructure development in Indonesia.

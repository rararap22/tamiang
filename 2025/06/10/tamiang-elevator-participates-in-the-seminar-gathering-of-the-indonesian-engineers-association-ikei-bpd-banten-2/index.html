Meeting of the Member Forum of the National Association of Indonesian Consultants – Aceh Province in 2024

  • tamiang
  • June 10, 2025

The Executive Committee of the Aceh Branch of the National Association of Indonesian Consultants (Inkindo) held a member forum (Fora) at the Hermes Palace Hotel, Banda Aceh, on Thursday, July 18, 2024.

In his speech, the Chairman of the Aceh Provincial Board of Inkindo, Safri Ismi, expressed his gratitude to the Governor of Aceh, who was represented by the Head of the Development Administration Bureau of the Aceh Regional Secretariat, T. Robby Irza. Safri further explained that the forum aimed to discuss various programs and identify issues frequently faced by consultants in the field.

Tamiang Elevator participated in the event as part of its commitment to supporting infrastructure development in Aceh. Tamiang Elevator’s participation in this forum not only demonstrated the company’s support for consultants but also provided an opportunity to share experiences and solutions related to elevator and escalator technology that can support more efficient and safer construction projects. Tamiang Elevator’s involvement is expected to make a positive contribution to improving the quality and safety of development projects in Aceh.
